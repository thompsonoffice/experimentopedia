# Transmuted experiments (Stitch! anime)

In the Stitch! anime (2008 to 2011), Hämsterviel transmuted many of Jumba's experiments into stronger, evil minions, usually leaving a yellow H mark on the forehead. In season 3, Delia altered experiments further; those episodes are titled with "2.0". The anime is generally treated as non-canon to the Western series. Only experiments whose sources describe a changed form are listed; each page has a section called Transmuted or altered form.
