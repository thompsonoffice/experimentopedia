# Experiment 412: Char

- Number: 412
- Name: Char
- Series: 4-Series: Top secret and mysterious series of militaristic and mostly failed experiments
- Pod color: Green
- First listed in: 212, Stitch!
- How much is known: partial

## Summary

An unseen experiment designed to burn bagels. Was mentioned in "Link" when Reuben was searching the experiment database. Seen in pod form in Stitch!.

An unseen experiment designed to burn bagels. Was mentioned in "Link" when Reuben was searching the experiment database. Seen in pod form in Stitch!.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: It has pyrokinesis and is programmed to burn food. That list gives its debut as Lilo & Stitch: The Series.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.

## Episode guide appearances

Episodes whose guides (liloandstitch.fandom.com) list this experiment:

### Link (episode) (Lilo & Stitch: The Series, season 2, episode 26)

When Lilo and Mertle are forced to work together to find buried treasure, an experiment that binds together uncooperative people with sticky elastic glue appears. Mertle helps Lilo catch Link, while Stitch becomes stuck to Nani and Jumba becomes stuck to Pleakley.

### Stitch's Birthday, Part Two (Stitch! anime, season 3, episode 10)

Yuna, Stitch, and Pleakley have messed up the timeline by traveling to the past, and Jumba must finish building Stitch before it is too late.
