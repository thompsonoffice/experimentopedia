# Experiment 274: Alexander

- Number: 274
- Name: Alexander
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: 137
- How much is known: partial

## Summary

An experiment designed to make lots of long distance telephone calls. He makes calls on your phone to run up the cost on your phone bill. One of the 12 new pods found by Lilo & Stitch in "Drowsy". His name is obviously taken from Alexander Graham Bell, the inventor of the telephone.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: He constantly makes expensive calls from his victim’s phone, eventually leaving her without money. It is named after Alexander Bell. That list gives its debut as Lilo & Stitch: The Series.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.

Lilo is in a hurry and all over the place. She is running late for hula class after finding twelve new experiment pods for Jumba to catalog. However, he himself is too busy with other things, so he leave the pods with Pleakley to catalog.
