## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: He is capable of making fake money. Jumba conceived it as an improved version of Snozzle, but Rich turned weak-willed, so he is only dangerous in the team with a 172-under. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
