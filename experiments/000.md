# Experiment 000: Zero

- Number: 000
- Name: Zero
- Series: 0-Series: Jumba's test batch, including many household helpers
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Stitch!
- How much is known: well-documented

## Summary

Primary function: Ultimate killing monster.

Experiment 000 (pronounced as "Experiment Zero"), or simply Zero, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and his earliest experiment before he began working with Hämsterviel and numbering his genetic creations. He is designed to be an ultimate killing monster and has enhanced his body with cybernetics at some point after escaping imprisonment. He appears as a minor antagonist in the second season of the Stitch! anime.

## Appearance

Zero's resemblance was close to a purple version of Stitch. Originally 100% organic, Zero is now a cyborg, part machine, part alien. His organic body is now black while the rest is mostly metal alloys and reinforced plastic. His form resembles a cross between a type of lizard and koala (and robot). He has a two cyborg arms, three spider-like legs, and half-helmet with a cyclonic-like right eye from some unknown incident. In cyborg form, he is missing his nose.

## Powers and abilities

In his original form, Zero has all the powers of Stitch. In Zero's cyborg form, his cyborg arms serve many purposes, such as cybernetic wiry rope, laser guns, a forked harpoon and welding tool, and his cyborg eye has zoom functions and improves his aim. He can manufacture many weapons, such as android minions, spaceships, and a doomsday device (supernova warhead).

## Personality

As Jumba created him to be the ultimate killing monster, Zero is ruthless, brutal, cruel, ambitious, destructive, and sadistic. Zero's programming is like Stitch's before he abandoned his destructive ways, which is to destroy everything in his path. However, Zero is proven to be too powerful and rebellious to control even for Jumba, which led him to suspend Zero and imprison him on an icy planet. Following his escape, Zero set out his objective to destroy the galaxy. Due to being on the icy planet, Zero didn't know he had experiments that were created after him, such as meeting Stitch and asking him who he is. However, Zero is remorseless and disregards the notion of family, having refused to listen to Stitch and his values on the subject. This is exemplified when Zero tried to attack Jumba after he was created, as well as when he built an army of robots to destroy the galaxy. When Zero launched his supernova warhead to destroy the galaxy, he didn't even care he would be caught in the explosion as well.

Zero is also very confident in his abilities, as he took on the Galactic Armada with his robot army and even faced off against Stitch himself. However, his confidence borders on overconfidence and arrogance, as well as making him reckless. When Zero destroyed Stitch's spaceship, his ship crashed into Zero's. When Zero launched his supernova warhead, he felt sure that Stitch wasn't going to stop it, and even when he redirected it in a black hole, Zero claimed that Stitch wouldn't survive. However, both of his claims proved wrong, as Stitch not only saved the galaxy, but also escaped the explosion.

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Stitch!

He was the first experiment created before Jumba started assigning numbers to his experiments, though Jumba later refers to him as "Experiment Zero" in his eponymous debut. Jumba was attempting to create the ultimate killer monster, and he was successful. However, his success also resulted in him being unable to control the experiment. Eventually, Jumba was forced to suspended Zero's cell activity and imprison him on an icy planet. Zero was eventually able to escape and acquired mechanical body parts, making him even more powerful, and became a ruthless warlord with a cyborg army in the galaxy. When Zero targets Hanpipi Nebula, the Grand Councilwoman contacts Stitch to help defeat him. Zero puts up a fight at first, but he was eventually defeated and captured by Stitch. Once restrained, Zero reveals he has a supernova warhead and then initiates its launch toward the Grand Council's headquarters on Planet Turo, threatening the entire galaxy. Stitch was able to redirect the warhead into a black hole forming nearby, saving everyone. It is unknown what happened to Zero and his cyborg army after his defeat.

## Trivia

- Voice: Ben Diskin (English dub); Tomohiro Nishimura (Japanese)
- Zero is the first experiment to be created by Jumba before he began working with Hämsterviel and numbering his genetic experiments, so he is classified as Experiment 000. He is referred to as "Experiment Zero" instead of "Experiment Zero-Zero-Zero", given that the number is a single digit rather than a whole three-digit.
- There is a misconception that Zero is named "Cyber". This moniker was never the experiment's actual name in any official Lilo & Stitch media. It is likely that fans came up with a unique moniker for him before it was made clear that he was never given a name by Disney.
- Zero is similar to Dark End but not different from 627 and Leroy, as while the latter pair were unable to be rehabilitated, the former pair were never mentioned to be able to be rehabilitated. Regardless, Zero and Dark End most likely would be unable to reform, as Zero proved to be uncontrollable after being created, while Dark End is loyal to Delia.
- Like 627 and Leroy, Zero and Dark End also serve as dark versions of Stitch, with Zero being too difficult to control where he disregarded the notion of family, Dark End having a low opinion on family and friends, and both 627 and Leroy being extremely cruel and destructive monsters that cannot be rehabilitated. 627, Leroy, Zero, and Dark End all serve as what Stitch would have become if he had never met Lilo and her ʻohana and been introduced to the meaning of family.
- Due to being imprisoned on an icy planet for countless years, Zero didn't know that there were experiments created after him, as he was surprised to see Stitch and asked him who he is.
- Zero (in cyborg form), Shrink, and Pix are the only known experiments whose legs resemble a tripod.
- He is one of the few experiments that are actually villains, along with Chopsuey, 627, Leroy and his clones, and Dark End, while all the other experiments were following their primary function.
