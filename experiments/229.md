# Experiment 229: Kingpin

- Number: 229

- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific

## Summary

Kingpin, also known as Experiment 229, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is an unlucky experiment designed to be a target for military war games.

## Appearance

Kingpin is an experiment that looks like a yellow bowling pin with rabbit ears, black eyes, a white stomach and a big blue nose.

## Powers and abilities

Kingpin is designed to be a target for military war games, and he is presumably also unlucky, which therefore makes him a difficult target.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: An improved version of Target. It looks like a yellow bowl with ears. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
