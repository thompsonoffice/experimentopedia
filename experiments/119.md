# Experiment 119: Inverto

- Number: 119
- Name: Inverto
- Series: 1-Series: Civic disturbances
- Pod color: Blue
- First listed in: 119, Leroy & Stitch
- How much is known: partial

## Summary

Experiment 054 (Fudgy), an experiment who drowns individuals into his sticky, chocolaty sweetness; was mistakenly labeled 119 and misunderstood as Experiment 611 because his pod was inverted. An ironic coincidence given Experiment 119's code name.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: An early and completely useless version of Experiment 611. Once Homjaxville thought that Gantu had sent him this experiment, but in fact there was an error in the database, and Homjaxville received experiment 054 (which, however, he also did not need). That list gives its debut as Lilo & Stitch: The Series.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
