# Experiment 228: Melty

- Number: 228
- Name: Melty
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific

- How much is known: well-documented

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 228th experiment; Liquefier.

Melty, also known as Experiment 228, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to liquefy enemy fortresses, weapons, and transportation by firing blue energy blasts that melt any solid object when hit. His one true place is melting metal at the aluminum recycling plant.

## Appearance

Melty is a small red dragon-like experiment with a long serpentine body. He has small bat-like wings, a long devilish tail, and a reptilian head with black eyes and thin-pointed antennae with dark-red markings. He also has tan patches on his stomach, chest, and around his eyes.

## Powers and abilities

Melty can fire blue balls of energy blasts from his snout that cause anything they hit to melt like chocolate. These energy blasts are extremely heated to the point that they will remain lethal, even when shot underwater. He can also fly.

## Personality

Melty is a rather simple-minded experiment, taking enjoyment in melting anything in sight, even when he is captured. One of his great advantages is that he will not be rendered powerless if his snout gets wet, as he is shown to be able to attack while underwater. He is also shown to be easily irritated and rather blunt, but is loyal to those who win his trust.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Melting stuff
- Dislikes: Being captured

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 228 was the 228th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to use his blue energy blasts to melt enemy targets. 228 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai, with 228's pod landing in a palm tree.

When a gust of wind blew 228's pod out of the tree, a dog found it on the ground and took it back to its home. There, the dog dropped the pod into its bowl of water, activating the experiment. The dog growled at 228 who proceeded to melt the house, scaring the dog away.

228 later attacked the parking lot near the Birds of Paradise Hotel while Stitch gave chase. He soon captured 228 using a metal chimney cap from the roof of the hotel.

Prior to catching 228, named Melty, Lilo got knocked into a mud puddle by Melty and made an idiot out of herself in front of Keoni. She and Stitch later learned about Jumba's time-traveling surfboard and decided to use it to go back in time and change the past.

In several alternate timelines created by Lilo and Stitch meddling with time travel, Melty's fate was different:
*In one timeline, Lilo barely avoided embarrassing herself by falling in the pool of mud, but Stitch's change of action caused Melty to melt the hotel, and he was caught in a fashion similar to Sparky. As a result, Nani was promptly fired from her job, causing Lilo and Stitch to start over.
*In the next timeline, Lilo easily avoided her previous mistake, but when Stitch tried to react his original steps, he accidentally threw off the balance of a falling tree, which destroyed Officer Kahiko's car, resulting in Lilo and Stitch being thrown in prison.
*In the third timeline, Lilo caused a chain reaction: she placed her hand against a palm tree and startled a butterfly, causing it to fly above the ocean where a fish jumped up and ate it; an eagle then snatched the fish and dropped it onto a ship's windshield, which startled the captain who then fell backward onto a green button, causing his cruise ship to crash into the hotel, but Melty's fate was unknown in this timeline.
*In the fourth timeline, Melty's fate was unknown, as Stitch forgot to turn on the paradox inhibitor, therefore causing him and Lilo to meet themselves.
*In the fifth timeline, Melty was unintentionally hurled into the ocean, where his melting blasts opened up a volcanic fissure in the ocean floor, causing a massive tsunami to form and destroy part of Kauai.
*In the sixth timeline, Melty was captured exactly like in the original, but Stitch caused a chain reaction this time: he blew off a feather that fell onto his arm, causing the feather to land on an unstable coconut, which fell off its pile and bounced down a hill onto a golf tee, where it got whacked into the air by an elderly golfer and hit the roof of Gantu's ship with a loud thud, waking Gantu up. When Experiment 625 revealed Melty's activation to Gantu, the latter then searched for the experiment at the hotel and wrecked it before coming to the Pelekais' house. After Melty melted his containment orb and their entire house, Gantu recaptured him. The time-traveling surfboard was melted as well, causing future alternate timeline Jumba (whom Lilo and Stitch accidentally met when unintentionally going forward in time) to go back and give them a new time-traveling surfboard.
*In the final timeline, and one corresponding to the original, Lilo and Stitch simply imitated their previous actions, but this made Lilo forcibly embarrass herself in front of Keoni again. They captured Melty the same way as before and took him home. Stitch then guarded Melty like a hawk in case Gantu decided to show up and rip off the roof of the house like he did in the previous timeline. Keoni even paid Lilo a visit to cheer her up, and thus everything turned out better than they had expected.

In the original and final timelines (in which Gantu slept peacefully and was never informed about Melty's activation), Melty was given a one true place burning metal at the aluminum recycling plant.

In "Remmy", Melty was one of the experiments in Lilo's dream.

### Leroy & Stitch

The first 624 experiments, including Melty, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

Melty participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones by shooting attacking Leroys with blue energy blasts.

However, the Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe," which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe.

### Stitch!

Melty made an appearance in the Stitch! anime special Stitch and the Planet of Sand.

## Trivia

- Voice: Frank Welker
- Melty greatly resembles Mushu from Mulan in appearance, color, and that they are both dragons. However, unlike Melty, Mushu lacks wings and cannot fly.
- Melty, Bugby, Babyfier, Jam, Wishy-Washy, Snooty, Amnesio, Hunkahunka, Blake, Slugger, Witch, and two unconfirmed experiments are the only known experiments that have wings.
- Melty, Yang, Heat, Plasmoid, and Splodyhead are the only known experiments whose powers are related to fire.
- Melty's pod color is green.
- The Experiment Pod Container describes Melty as: "Experiment 228. Primary function: liquefaction of enemy fortresses, weaponry, and transportation."
- Melty's body, ears, wings, and tail appear in the online game Jumba's Lab.
