# Experiment 531: Hisee

- Number: 531
- Name: Hisee
- Series: 5-Series: Elemental and environmental manipulators
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Disney Adventures Magazines
- How much is known: partial

## Summary

A green mole/chimp-like experiment with six hairs on its head, a very small body and a stubby tail. It appeared in some Disney Adventures comics (the ones where Jumba is creating Stitch). Its function is unknown.

## Appearance and abilities (from the experiment list)

Not stated in canon.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: (Comics) An improved version of Snafu. It is also intended to disrupt all sorts of plans, but is smarter than its predecessor and is attentive to the events of the past.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
