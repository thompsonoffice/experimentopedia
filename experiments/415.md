## Summary

An unseen experiment designed to eat a single sock in each pair. Was mentioned in "Link" when Reuben was searching the experiment database.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: They eat socks, and each pair eats only one sock. That list gives its debut as Lilo & Stitch: The Series.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.

## Episode guide appearances

Episodes whose guides (liloandstitch.fandom.com) list this experiment:

### Link (episode) (Lilo & Stitch: The Series, season 2, episode 26)

When Lilo and Mertle are forced to work together to find buried treasure, an experiment that binds together uncooperative people with sticky elastic glue appears. Mertle helps Lilo catch Link, while Stitch becomes stuck to Nani and Jumba becomes stuck to Pleakley.
