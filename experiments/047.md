# Experiment 047: Lorider

- Number: 047

## Summary

Lorider, also known as Experiment 047, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to publicly humiliate victims by pulling down their pants (hence the name Lo-Rider). Jumba would often release him in crowds and meetings. He appears in the Stitch! anime.

## Appearance

Lorider is a blue platypus-like experiment with small eyes and a purple accordion-like body.

## Powers and abilities

Lorider can extend his accordion-like body to stretch up to someone's waist, and then he pulls down their pants.

Lorider makes quacking noises when he jumps, stretches, or walks.

## Personality

Lorider delights in humiliating victims, and he won't stop embarrassing them until he gets the full effect. He also acts somewhat like a dog, sniffing the target before embarrassing them.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Debagging (pantsing) victims; Causing humiliation

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Original purpose and background

Experiment 047 was the 47th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to yank down victims' pants, causing humiliation to the victim. 047 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba on his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Stitch!

In "Stitch's Birthday, Part One", Lorider was activated, pulled a public speaker's pants down, and when the speaker pulled them up and started to run, Lorider tripped him. Lorider then pulled down the pants of various participants until he was eventually captured by Jumba.

## Trivia

- Voice: Keith Silverstein
- His name in Japanese is ローライダー, which translates directly to Lowrider (a literal translation of its name).
- Lorider's pod color is green.
- Lorider greatly resembles Perry the Platypus from Phineas and Ferb.
- Lorider is not identified as an experiment in the English dub credits of his only appearance, instead only being credited as "Alien Platypus". It is unknown why the credits removed his proper identity.
- Lorider and Sproing are the only known experiments that can extend their bodies.
