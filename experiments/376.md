# Experiment 376: Cassandra

- Number: 376
- Name: Cassandra
- Series: 3-Series: Psychological
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Leroy & Stitch

## Summary

Little canonical info: this experiment is listed in official sources (often only in the Leroy & Stitch finale lineup, end credits, or the Stitch! anime) but the shows and films never explain what it looks like or does. Nothing has been invented to fill the gap.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: An enhanced version of Hunkahunka. Seeing two people holding hands, she shoots them with resin, which causes them to immediately fall in love and head to the registry office. The resin (and effect) can be removed by dousing victims' hands with dirty water. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
