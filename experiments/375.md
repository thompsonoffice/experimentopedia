# Experiment 375: Phantasmo

- Number: 375
- Name: Phantasmo
- Series: 3-Series: Psychological
- Pod color: Yellow
- First listed in: 110
- How much is known: well-documented

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 375th experiment; Havoc wreaker; Entertainer.

Phantasmo, also known as Experiment 375, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to take possession of inanimate objects and, through them, create utter chaos. Being ghost-like, he is intangible and can phase through obstacles, thus making him virtually uncatchable. His one true place is keeping the Macki Macaw animatronic functional at the titular pizza restaurant.

## Appearance

Phantasmo is a green, semi-transparent ghostlike experiment with short arms, a large conehead with two light green stripes, a koala-like face, long rabbit-like ears, and dark pupil-less eyes. Instead of legs, Phantasmo has a continuous wisp-like tail.

## Powers and abilities

Phantasmo's spectral nature gives him abilities commonly associated with those of spirits. He can possess any inanimate object and bring it to life and can also phase through anything, making him impossible to keep in one place. As a ghostly experiment, he is virtually uncatchable by conventional means (including nets and capsules), thanks to his intangibility.

## Personality

Phantasmo is a mischievous experiment who enjoys wreaking havoc. He also has a tendency to gloat by laughing in a squeaky voice and is rather quite smart too. When possessing an object, he tries to do so when nobody is around and will act as the object he is possessing to avoid being exposed. Due to advanced programming, he would dispose of those who discover his true identity. Being always on the move, jumping from object to object, he craves attention. This is ultimately exploited in order to get him to perform for attention instead of causing chaos.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Bringing inanimate objects to life, entertaining children, attention
- Dislikes: Revealing himself to others, being feared or hated

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 375 was the 375th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to possess inanimate objects and cause massive mischief and chaos through them. 375 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

375's pod eventually made its way into a pearl oyster inside the crane game at Macki Macaw's pizzeria. Stitch played the claw game and won the oyster, but a glitch in the machine caused him to shake it in frustration, prompting Nani to intervene and scold Stitch. After the oyster freed itself, despite Stitch's misconduct, the employee let Lilo keep the prize, and she and Stitch took it home with them.

When Lilo tried to give Stitch a bath (after Nani mentioned the word "bath"), the latter fled and hid in the laundry hamper, but she tricked Stitch into coming out and then dragged him to the bathroom. There, an incident with the bathtub, which overflowed after Lilo immersed Stitch in the water, resulted in the oyster opening and the pod inside getting wet, activating 375.

That night, 375 wreaked havoc in the kitchen. When Stitch was alerted, he attempted to save Pleakley's dishes, but 375 made Stitch lose his balance and shatter them. 375 then possessed Scrump and used the doll to paint Nani's face green, sabotage Jumba's experiment analyzer, steal Pleakley's begonia, break Lilo's record player, brush Nani's toothbrush on Stitch's toes, and frame Stitch for it all.

The next morning, Nani, thinking Stitch was the culprit, threw the latter outside and made Lilo chain him up as punishment until Nani returned from work. However, Stitch noticed 375 using Scrump again to unravel Lilo's Elvis videotapes. Stitch broke free and attacked Lilo's doll, only to arouse her suspicion. When Stitch was later being prosecuted, he saw 375 (still inside Scrump) wink at him, causing Stitch to lash out at Lilo's doll.

Later that day, while 375 was attempting to break Pleakley's flower pot to get Stitch into deeper trouble, Lilo caught him right in the act. 375 revealed himself and possessed a rope, with which it tied Lilo, Jumba, and Pleakley up. He then repossessed Scrump and prepared to execute the three with Jumba's blaster, but a wary and vigilant Stitch ambushed 375 and freed his ʻohana.

While the four searched for 375, he possessed a vacuum cleaner and almost sucked Pleakley up with it. Lilo and Stitch then made a vain attempt to capture the intangible experiment, forcing 375 to retreat into Kokaua Town.

After a long pursuit through town, when 375 turned the tables, Nani gave Lilo and Stitch a lift in her jeep and led 375 to Macki Macaw's, where he used the claw machine to trap Stitch. The former then chased Lilo backstage and possessed the Macki Macaw animatronic that had malfunctioned earlier. After Lilo raised the curtain, 375 put on a show for an audience.

375, christened Phantasmo, was then convinced by Lilo that using the animatronic parrot to entertain children and receive adoration was a better way of gaining attention than causing chaos, thus rehabilitating him now that he had found his one true place.

Phantasmo made a cameo at the end of "Snooty" on Gantu's TV set continuing his duty inside the Macki Macaw animatronic as the ex-captain watched the show with great interest.

### Leroy & Stitch

The first 624 experiments, including Phantasmo, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

Phantasmo participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones, but it is unknown what he did. Although he was seen among some of the Leroys, it is presumed that he wouldn't have been able to be very effective, due to the fact that his powers are deemed useless.

The Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle, but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe", which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe.

### Stitch!

Phantasmo made a special guest appearance in the Stitch! segment "Kung Fu Dragon Pleak Lee".

## Trivia

- Voice: Nancy Cartwright
- Phantasmo was first revealed in a DVD bonus feature of Stitch! The Movie in the experiment gallery.
- An early version of Phantasmo appears on an old downloadable activity printout for Stitch! The Movie. This beta design depicts him with a rounder Stitch-like head, a round nose, tufts of fur on his head and chest, and two short antennae.
- This design is confirmed to be him through storyboards of his episode by storyboard artist Wendy Grieb.
- It is likely that his design was changed because he resembled Stitch too much.
- Phantasmo is clearly a spectral being, as he possesses intangibility and the ability to phase through obstacles, thus making him the only experiment that cannot be restrained or captured.
- Due to this, it is unknown how Jumba managed to create him, let alone dehydrate him into an experiment pod.
- This would come into question in Leroy & Stitch on how he was captured by a Leroy clone. Also, he was seen being chased by another Leroy clone with a plasma gun, despite his inability to sustain physical injuries.
- Phantasmo, Fudgy, Remmy, Tickle-Tummy, and Witch are the only known experiments to have arms but no legs.
- Interestingly, excluding Fudgy, all of these experiments are capable of levitation. Remmy is always seen levitating. Phantasmo and Witch primarily levitate but often possess objects or living beings to move by other means. Tickle-Tummy is also able to levitate, but has been seen bouncing as well.
- Phantasmo, Glitch, and Witch are all capable of possession, albeit in different capacities: Phantasmo can possess any inanimate object, Glitch possesses technology, and Witch can possess living beings.
- Phantasmo has the ability to speak fluently, but he only does so after possessing the Macki Macaw animatronic. Also, he replaces the animatronic's pre-recorded voice with that of his own and inexplicably knows who Macki Macaw and the Hammerheads are.
- Due to being voiced by Nancy Cartwright, his voice is similar to Bart Simpson.
- As Phantasmo's possession of objects brings them to life, the possessed objects are able to perform actions that they normally cannot do.
- Despite being lightweight and having a flexible body, Scrump was able to not only move "herself", but "she" also gains the strength to lift or pull objects, such as a cloth that had Pleakley's plant on it. The same goes to a bunch of bananas in the Pelekais' kitchen that Phantasmo possesses shortly after his activation, as he uses them to open a microwave oven's door.
- A traffic light sprouted arms and legs, grabbing some coconuts and running off until Stitch runs it over with the X-Buggy, destroying it.
- A tiki statue from a store facade was able to move its own limbs and run off. Its eyes were also able to narrow and widen.
- The Macki Macaw animatronic was actually repaired by his possession and vastly upgraded, being able to fly without flapping its wings, play its ukulele, and speak independently (though with Phantasmo's own voice). The animatronic also became more lifelike and less robotic, as its exposed joints and bolts seemingly vanished after his possession.
- Phantasmo's pod color is yellow.
- Interestingly, electrical discharge can be seen when Phantasmo's pod gets wet and activates.
- Phantasmo's ears appear in the online game Jumba's Lab.
