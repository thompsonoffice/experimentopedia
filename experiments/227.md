## Summary

Butter, also known as Experiment 227, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to batter through thick doors and barricades.

## Appearance

Butter is a large, quadrupedal, tan mammoth-like experiment with a large head, four bulky legs with two external toes, two tiny little antennae, a big round green nose instead of a trunk, green markings on his back, a big hump near his face, and tusks as large as the entire front of his body.

## Powers and abilities

Butter can batter through thick doors by using his tusks and large head.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Trivia

- Voice: |films = Leroy & Stitch
- Butter, along with Gigi, Dorkifier, Nosox, Shortstuff, Spike, Yang, Richter, Phoon, Tank, Splodyhead, and one unconfirmed experiment, are the only known experiments that are primarily quadrupeds.
- Both Butter and the unconfirmed experiment (a large green Ankylosaurus/hippopotamus-like experiment with a big nose and mouth, two stubby antennae, and a large, spiked beaver-like tail) debuted in Leroy & Stitch.
- Butter's name was only confirmed via a storyboard drawing shared online by Tony Craig.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: It's designed to knock down doors. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
