# Experiment 600: Woops

- Number: 600
- Name: Woops
- Series: 6-Series: Battlefield and doomsday experiments with galactic implications
- Pod color: Blue
- First listed in: 224
- How much is known: rich

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 600th experiment; Bowler.

Woops, also known as Experiment 600, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is one of the failed prototypes of Stitch, possessing all the same powers, but turned out to be a real klutz; he accidentally knocks over and/or breaks anything he touches and only says, "Woops!" after doing so, hence his name. His one true place is at Kokaua Town Lanes as a champion bowler on Pleakley's bowling team, the Kokaua Bowling Brawlers.

## Appearance

Woops physically resembles Stitch, but with different body proportions. His body is bean-shaped, and he has long, thick limbs, an overbite exposing yellow buckteeth, a large Adam's-apple, a football-shaped head, a huge, bulbous nose, and three short pale mint green-tipped antennae and a tail. His ears and eyes are smaller than Stitch's.

## Powers and abilities

Woops has all the powers of Stitch, including super-strength and durability, but he is too clumsy and unbalanced to use them. As a result, he unintentionally destroys everything around him. He is skilled at bowling (one of the few things where knocking stuff over is a good thing).

## Personality

Woops is a very clumsy, accident-prone, and unbalanced experiment. He is easily guilty but never mourns over past accidents or incidents. He apologizes as often as he falls, and his personality is rather clownish and dim-witted. He is usually seen swaying side to side, laughing awkwardly, and picking his nose with his finger. He is left-footed and can get upset if abandoned or contained for periods of time.

An amiable sort, Woops merely wants to do the right thing but just can't seem to do it. He can hardly seem to stand on his own two feet, let alone keep his balance, and is always running into objects and knocking them over. Most individuals find him unbearably annoying, but he is a bowling champion.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Knocking things over, picking his nose, bowling, saying "Woops!"
- Dislikes: Being abandoned or contained

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 600 was the 600th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed for Stitch's purpose, being indestructible and capable of anything, but was instead a destructive klutz who did nothing except break everything in his path. 600 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

600's pod was found by Gantu and sent to Hämsterviel. When the latter discovered that 600 was a prototype of Stitch, Hämsterviel activated the experiment. However, he and the captive experiments were awestruck by 600's klutzy behavior around the prison, which alerted the guards and almost blew Hämsterviel's cover in the process.

As a result, Hämsterviel was forced to teleport 600 and the other captive experiments back to Earth on Gantu's ship, with orders to get rid of 600, as he gave Hämsterviel a headache, and to hold on to the other experiments until Hämsterviel was clear to hold them captive in his prison cell again. Meanwhile, 600's clumsiness around the ship ruined Gantu's azaleas, broke his blaster, and freed Nosy from containment, allowing him to escape.

Eventually, the mayhem caused by 600's clumsiness drove Gantu crazy enough to offer him to Lilo and Stitch. 600 was then taken in and named Woops by Lilo. Though initially dismayed by the clumsy experiment, Jumba discreetly suggested bringing Woops to Pleakley's domino tournament (in a quite evil voice). However, upon seeing Woops, Pleakley panicked and then demanded Lilo to lock the klutz away until the tournament was over.

Inside a containment orb at the Pelekai residence, Woops just so happened to sneeze, causing a chain reaction that transported him onto the streets of Kokaua Town. He soon started to bounce toward the dominoes.

Just before impact, Stitch caught Woops and then let the latter out of his container. Woops suddenly sneezed again, which caused Stitch to stagger backward into the Ice Cream Man, causing the ice cream from his cone to hit the dominoes, much to Pleakley's horror.

Towards the end of the domino toppling, a startled cat hit a trash can, causing another chain reaction of objects hitting each other until the old lighthouse was destroyed, plummeting into the ocean.

The events caused Lilo to discover that Woops was good at knocking things over (thanks to his clumsiness), and the latter found his one true place at the local bowling alley as a talented bowler (one of the few things where knocking stuff over is good) on Pleakley's bowling team, the Kokaua Bowling Brawlers.

In "Snafu", Nosy revealed to Lilo and Stitch that Woops blew the secret about Hämsterviel's captive experiments, so he sent them all back to Gantu. Nosy then added that he got lucky because his container broke thanks to Woops.

### Leroy & Stitch

The first 624 experiments, including Woops, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

Woops participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones, but it is unknown what he did.

The Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe", which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe.

## Trivia

- Voice: Jeff Bennett
- Woops is the first known prototype of Stitch and is a massive failure.
- Despite being a failure, Woops did help Stitch more than most other experiments: by freeing Nosy, he started the chain of events seen in "Snafu", which ended up with Lilo and Stitch saving all the other 17 experiments imprisoned by Hämsterviel, including Angel, whom Stitch was feeling the most guilt for letting her be captured by Gantu and shipped to Hämsterviel. Ironically, Woops never met Angel (due to Angel somehow not appearing in Woops' debut episode).
- Unlike most experiments, Woops' number is pronounced as a whole number ("six hundred") instead of three individual digits (which in his case would have been "six-oh-oh") as per the norm for the experiments.
- The only other experiment seen in the original series whose number is also referred to as a whole number is Spooky (Experiment 300). This implies that experiments whose number is divisible by one hundred (100) are the primary exceptions to the normal by-digit pronunciation of experiment numbers.
- Woops' pod color is blue.
- Woops is one of the few experiments to not appear in the group photo at the end of Leroy & Stitch.
- Woops' design bears a great resemblance to Sid from the films. Coincidentally, Disney currently owns the Ice Age franchise following the studio's acquisition of 20th Century Fox in 2019.

When Lilo and Stitch are informed by Nosy that all the experiments captured by Gantu are imprisoned in his ship, the two plan a rescue mission to free them. ### Woops (episode) (Lilo & Stitch: The Series, season 2, episode 24)

A clumsy experiment causes Dr. Hämsterviel to nearly be caught by his prison guards, so he returns all the captured experiments to Gantu for safekeeping. However, when Gantu eventually lets the troublemaking experiment go, Woops threatens to ruin Pleakley's chance at a domino championship.
