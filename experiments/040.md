# Experiment 040: Backhoe

- Number: 040

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 40th experiment; Digger.

Backhoe, also known as Experiment 040, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to be an efficient digger and scrape up vegetation.

## Appearance and abilities (from the experiment list)

A gray mole-like experiment with large black claws. Designed to scrape up vegetation, and is also an efficient digger. He was first seen in "Shoe," where he dug the pool for "Jumba & Pleakley's Bed & Not Breakfast," and was part of the "rebellion" in "Checkers."

## Powers and abilities

Digging capacity

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Digging
- Dislikes: Being captured

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 040 was the 40th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to dig up vegetation. 040 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

At an unknown point, 040 was presumably activated, captured by Lilo and Stitch, tamed, and named Backhoe.

In "Shoe", Backhoe was one of the experiments that helped turn Jumba's ship into a hotel.

Backhoe was one of the experiments in "Checkers" that joined Stitch in a rebellion against Gantu. Earlier in the episode, he, along with Yin and Digger, helped Lilo make mud.

In "Remmy", Backhoe was one of the experiments in Lilo's dream.

### The Origin of Stitch

Backhoe was one of the experiments that were gliding on the screen, but he is 569 instead of 040.

### Leroy & Stitch

The first 624 experiments, including Backhoe, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

Backhoe participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones.

The Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle, but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe", which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe.

## Trivia

- Voice: Frank Welker
- Backhoe was activated off-screen.
- Backhoe, Digger, and Derrick are the only known experiments whose powers are related to excavation.
- Backhoe is one of the few experiments to not have his one true place revealed.
- Backhoe seems to always be smiling, but he can be quite vicious as seen in Leroy & Stitch.
