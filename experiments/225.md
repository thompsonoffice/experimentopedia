# Experiment 225: Mashy

- Number: 225
- Name: Mashy
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific
- Pod color: Red
- First listed in: Leroy & Stitch, Stitch!
- How much is known: rich

## Summary

Mashy, also known as Experiment 225, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to crush precious orbs of nearly indestructible Uburnium to useless pulp. He does what Clip was intended to be designed to do: consume Uburnium to cause a fuel crisis.

## Appearance

Mashy is a light grey-blue dinosaur-like experiment, with a huge mouth, with tiny horns above each nostril, two large ears and a large body.

## Powers and abilities

Mashy can crush orbs of Uburnium with his enormous mouth.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Trivia

- Mashy appears in the Stitch! anime as a red pod labeled 225, but was never activated.
- Though unconfirmed, Clip can possibly be considered a prototype of Mashy.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: A devourer of uburnium, a popular fuel for spacecraft. Corrected version of Clip. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
