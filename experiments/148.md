## Summary

Weasel, also known as Experiment 148, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to crawl up planetary leaders' pants during public speeches.

## Appearance

Weasel is a brown weasel-like experiment with black eyes, red-orange stripes and tan eye patches and a tan stomach from the chin down.

## Powers and abilities

Weasel can climb up planetary leaders' pant legs during public appearances using sneak tactics, along with his size and obvious agility due to his weasel attributes.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: He is programmed to get on the feet of a world leader in his pants when he appears in public. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
