# Experiment 277: Snooty

- Number: 277
- Name: Snooty
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific
- Pod color: Red or White
- First listed in: 213 and 220
- How much is known: rich

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 277th experiment; Snootonium/mucus sucker.

Snooty, also known as Experiment 277, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to mine and enrich Snootonium, a rare intergalactic energy source that becomes unstable and dangerous once enriched. Luckily, there is none on Earth, which Gantu sees as no reason to capture Snooty, only doing so under Hämsterviel's orders. However, Snootonium has a similar chemical composition to mucus, and thus his programming draws him to individuals with large quantities. His one true place is as Victoria's pet, clearing her sinuses.

## Appearance

Snooty is a vampire-esque experiment resembling a light-purple bat. He has a furless, triangle-shaped body and pointed ears. He has stumpy legs and dark purple wings each with two fingers and a thumb. He hardly has a neck, but in place is a wide fanged mouth.

## Powers and abilities

To detect Snootonium, Snooty sees in an unidentified vision spectrum that allows him to detect the substance (similar to how snakes' infrared vision allows them to detect body heat). Snooty can also mine and enrich Snootonium and/or mucus by using his mouth/fangs; in the latter's case, he retrieves it via sucking it from a victim's nostrils in seconds.

## Personality

Snooty is a single-minded experiment dedicated to mining and enriching Snootonium. There is none on Earth, but Snootonium has a similar chemical composition to mucus, so his programming draws him to individuals with large quantities of mucus, such as Victoria and Stitch (his body is 62.7% mucus). Unlike most experiments, Snooty is not inherently vile and is one of the tamer experiments that will avoid harming others in his search. However, he will defend himself if he is provoked and is very persistent in fulfilling his primary function.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Snootonium/mucus, Victoria
- Dislikes: Being captured

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 277 was the 277th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to mine and enrich Snootonium, a rare element that has a similar chemical composition as Earth mucus. 277 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

277's pod eventually made its way onto a pickup truck's antenna. When the owner took the dirty pickup through a car wash, the pod got wet in the wash, activating the experiment. After spooking the pickup's driver, 277 left in search of mucus (considering the fact that there was no Snootonium available on Earth).

Drawn by her allergies, 277 frequently attacked Victoria, who mistook him for a real vampire, but each time he was warded off by Lilo and Stitch. They also prevented an impetuous Gantu from capturing the experiment, despite Victoria's protests. When Lilo later looked up 277 on Jumba's computer, she informed Victoria that 277 was not an actual vampire and didn't need to be slain or feared once rehabilitated, although Victoria remained wary.

Later, when Lilo and Victoria were out looking for 277, named Snooty, he made another attempt on Victoria, prompting her to hide in Officer Kahiko's police car. During this assault, Stitch bravely challenged the bat-like experiment. Snooty, upon detecting the visible mucus in Stitch's body, attacked the latter and sucked him dry. After Snooty put Stitch out of commission, Victoria vowed to slay the former if she had to do it without Lilo's help.

When Lilo brought a weak and fatigued Stitch home, Jumba explained that Stitch's body was 62.7% mucus that had been drained out, temporarily weakening him. Jumba then revealed that mucus had a chemical composition similar to Snootonium, despite it being unavailable on Earth. This also explained why Snooty kept going after Victoria.

Meanwhile, Victoria was in a hardware store looking for items to slay Snooty with. She then noticed Gantu using the store's TV set (since Hämsterviel had supposedly cut Gantu's cable service earlier according to Experiment 625) and decided to team up with him to slay the experiment. Using Gantu's shuttle, the two tracked Snooty down to his cave in the Hawaiian mountains, where Gantu used Victoria as bait to successfully draw Snooty out.

Lilo soon arrived with Pleakley and was shocked to see Victoria consorting with Gantu. She claimed that Gantu was helping her slay Snooty, but Lilo managed to convince Victoria of his treachery. Gantu blasted Snooty into a net, but the latter was then saved by Victoria and Lilo, who both fought Gantu for the experiment.

After being freed, Snooty rested contentedly in Victoria's arms. When Snooty used his mucus-sucking power to clear Victoria's sinuses, the latter kept him as a pet.

### Leroy & Stitch

In the film's opening, Snooty can be seen waving hello to Lilo, along with Babyfier and Amnesio.

Later, the first 624 experiments, including Snooty, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

Snooty participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones by dodging Leroys' plasma projectiles and possibly draining Leroys' mucus.

However, the Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle, but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe", which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe.

## Trivia

- Voice: Dee Bradley Baker
- Snooty is an obvious parody of  and vampires in general, except that vampires suck people's blood.
- Snooty, Bugby, Babyfier, Jam, Melty, Wishy-Washy, Amnesio, Hunkahunka, Blake, Slugger, Witch, and two unconfirmed experiments are the only known experiments that have wings.
- Snooty and Jam are the only known experiments based on bats. Interestingly, they are both in the 2-series and shades of purple.
- Victoria initially mistook Snooty for a real vampire, thus fearing him and wanting him dead.
- Snooty is one of the few known experiments whose powers Stitch is vulnerable to.
- Snooty is the first and only known experiment that Victoria caught.
- He is also one of the six known experiments to be rehabilitated by someone other than Lilo and Stitch, the other five being Gigi, Elastico, Yaarp, Spike, and Frenchfry.
- Snooty, in particular, was rehabilitated by Victoria.
- Snooty's pod color is red. However, a white pod labeled 277 appeared among other pods in "Mrs. Hasagawa's Cats".
- The Experiment Pod Container describes Snooty as: "Experiment 277. Primary function: Mine and enrich Snootonium."
- He is the only known experiment whose above primary function is incapable of being utilized on Earth, as Snootonium is a substance not found on Earth.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: It is designed to enrich snutonium, a rare metal that becomes explosive when enriched. In the absence of snutonia feeds on human snot. She lives with Victoria. That list gives its debut as Lilo & Stitch: The Series.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.

## Episode guide appearances

Episodes whose guides (liloandstitch.fandom.com) list this experiment:

### Mrs. Hasagawa's Cats (Lilo & Stitch: The Series, season 2, episode 23)

Lilo and Stitch try to help Mrs. Hasagawa out by organizing her fruit stand and cleaning her yard. However, when she tries to offer them a bowl of apricots for their help, they discover that the bowl is actually full of experiment pods, and her house is later full of stray experiments, which she believes are "cats". Lilo and Stitch try to gather all the experiments, but it turns out that Mrs. Hasagawa has taken a liking to them and tamed them herself, so the experiments won't need to be captured after all.

### Snooty (episode) (Lilo & Stitch: The Series, season 2, episode 10)

While Lilo, Stitch, and Victoria are having fun taking zombie pictures, an experiment pod activates, unleashing a bat-like experiment on the island. By evening, the experiment finds its way to Victoria's and tries to attack her. Victoria believes its a vampire, and calls Lilo and Stitch for help, who arrive with their vampire hunting gear.
