# Experiment 340: Nodesserto

- Number: 340
- Name: Nodesserto
- Series: 3-Series: Psychological
- Pod color: Green
- First listed in: 118, Stitch!
- How much is known: partial

## Summary

Designed to cause mass depression by stealing puddings, cakes, pies, and other sweets. His name is often forgotten, and so he is called "Whatsisname" (revealed in the credits of Leroy & Stitch). Caught by Gantu. Was mentioned by Hämsterviel in "Amnesio". Seen in pod form in Stitch!.
