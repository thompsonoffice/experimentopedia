# Experiment 255: Mrs. Sickly

- Number: 255
- Name: Mrs. Sickly
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific
- Pod color: Blue
- First listed in: Stitch!
- How much is known: rich

## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 255th experiment; Stink bomb (near the end).

Mrs. Sickly, also known as Experiment 255, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. She is designed to make people sick with her nauseatingly sweet fumes. She appears in the Stitch! anime.

## Appearance

Mrs. Sickly is a light teal Mr. Stenchy-like experiment with golden eyes, puffier antennae and smaller hair.

## Powers and abilities

Like Mr. Stenchy, Mrs. Sickly appears disarmingly cute to everyone else except other experiments, but then emits an extremely foul and noxious odor 48 hours after she is activated. Additionally, if Mrs. Sickly's emissions intermix with Mr. Stenchy's sour emissions, the chemical reaction will cause a cataclysmic explosion capable of destroying the planetary body they inhabit.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Original purpose and background

Experiment 255 was the 255th genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. She was designed to make anyone sick with her nauseatingly sweet fumes. 255 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Stitch!

Mrs. Sickly first appeared when Hämsterviel released her along with Mr. Stenchy during Christmas in Japan. As part of his plot, Hämsterviel mass-produced clones of Mr. Stenchy and Mrs. Sickly and sold them to many children across Japan and the rest of the world. This is when it is revealed that if the emissions from these two mix, they have the power to destroy entire planets. This leads to Yuna, Pleakley, Stitch, Jumba, Hämsterviel, and Gantu on a hunt to round up the clones of Mr. Stenchy and Mrs. Sickly in order to save the world's Christmas. After saving the world's Christmas, Mrs. Sickly (and Mr. Stenchy) was dehydrated back into an experiment pod and locked in Jumba's vault. Eventually, Jumba grabbed her pod to help Stitch in his fight against Dark End, but Mrs. Sickly wasn't one of the experiments that was activated. She also appeared in the special Stitch and the Planet of Sand.

## Trivia

- Voice: Michelle Ruff
- Mrs. Sickly is one of the rare experiments to have sclerae and pupils instead of solid black eyes, the others being Mr. Stenchy, Skip, Clink, Nosox, Bragg, Thresher, Tippy, Slobber, and four unconfirmed experiments.
- While it is confirmed that Mrs. Sickly is Mr. Stenchy's wife, it remains unconfirmed whether her one true place is on Plorgonar with her husband.
- Mrs. Sickly's pod color is blue.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: It gives off poisonous steam, which makes everyone sick. She lives with Mr. Zapaschko in Porgonar, where her ability is harmless. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
