# Experiment 425: Mr. Brown

- Number: 425
- Name: Mr. Brown
- Series: 4-Series: Top secret and mysterious series of militaristic and mostly failed experiments
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Leroy & Stitch
- How much is known: function only (unverified)

## Summary

Little canonical info: this experiment is listed in official sources (often only in the Leroy & Stitch finale lineup, end credits, or the Stitch! anime) but the shows and films never explain what it looks like or does. Nothing has been invented to fill the gap.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: A socially useful experiment to combat racists. His victim gets a wild fear of brown for 24 hours, and when it passes, the victim realizes the fallacy of racism. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
