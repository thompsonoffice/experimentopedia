# Experiment 568: Slenky

- Number: 568
- Name: Slenky
- Series: 5-Series: Elemental and environmental manipulators
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Leroy & Stitch
- How much is known: function only (unverified)

## Summary

Little canonical info: this experiment is listed in official sources (often only in the Leroy & Stitch finale lineup, end credits, or the Stitch! anime) but the shows and films never explain what it looks like or does. Nothing has been invented to fill the gap.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: It was conceived as a universal elementalist (like Crystal in the Marvel Universe), but Jumba mixed up several DNA samples when he created it. Sleenki has pyrokinesis, superhuman speed, bulletproof skin and indestructible bones, and can also deliver heavy-duty blows with its hammer-like nose. The weakness is the same as Stitch's: he can't swim. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
