# Experiment 220: Millie

- Number: 220
- Name: Millie
- Series: 2-Series: Technological and scientific
- Pod color: Not shown
- First listed in: Leroy & Stitch
- How much is known: rich

## Summary

Millie, also known as Experiment 220, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. She is designed to plug up air holes in filters crucial to all hyperdrives.

## Appearance

Millie is a giant green millipede-like experiment with a koala-like face, a wide mouth, a big blue nose, black eyes, Nosy-like ears and ten short tentacle-like limbs. Her legs are stubby and dotted with black toes.

## Powers and abilities

Millie can plug holes in air filters by using her long tentacle-like arms.

## One true place

Not stated in canon.

## Trivia

- Millie and Yin are the only two experiments with the most limbs out of any known experiments, with ten and eleven respectively. Millie has eight tentacle-like arms and two tentacle-like legs, while Yin has eight tentacles, two tiny arms, and one large slug-like foot.

## Reported function (unverified fan source)

A Russian-language fan list (disney-classic.ru, machine-translated) describes it this way: An improved version of Missy. That list gives its debut as Leroy & Stitch.

This is not confirmed by Disney or the English wikis, so treat it as unverified.
