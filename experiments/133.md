## Summary

Primary function: Jumba's 133rd experiment; Prankster.

PJ, also known as Experiment 133, is an illegal genetic experiment created by Jumba Jookiba and a character in the Lilo & Stitch franchise. He is designed to play practical jokes on unsuspecting victims. His one true place is performing an opening act for Moses' hula gig.

## Appearance

PJ is an orange and tan koala-like experiment with a white face and white belly, a wide mouth, red nose, black eyes, two thin yellow-tipped antennae on his head resembling rabbit-like ears, and a functioning blow-horn for a tail (reminiscent of 's trademark horn), which he honks instead of speaking. He wears joke eyeglasses with a fake nose and mustache resembling those of .

## Powers and abilities

PJ is a skilled practical joker. He can play practical jokes by using his surroundings, various joke gags (e.g. hand buzzer, whoopee cushion, squirt flower, etc.), and other things he finds.

## Personality

PJ is incredibly silly and mischievous, even towards other experiments. Being an expert prankster, PJ uses his intelligence to create ingenious pranks. As Lilo describes, PJ can be annoying with his pranks, and while his pranks can be mean, he doesn't seem to be purposely malicious, but merely carries out his programming. Like most experiments, he dislikes it when his goals do not carry through or are interrupted. He likes making individuals laugh with his practical jokes.

## Likes and dislikes

- Likes: Playing jokes and pranks
- Dislikes: Being captured, his jokes backfiring

## Appearances and how Lilo and Stitch met it

### Lilo & Stitch: The Series

Experiment 133 was the 133rd genetic experiment created by Jumba with Hämsterviel's funding. He was designed to play practical jokes on victims, hence his name P(ractical) J(oker). 133 and the other first 624 experiments were deactivated and smuggled to Earth by Jumba during his mission to capture Experiment 626.

All of the experiment pods were released and scattered across the island of Kauai.

When a man using a metal detector on the beach uncovered an empty soda can with 133's pod, he decided that the pod wasn't worth anything, so he threw it into the water, activating the experiment. 133, named PJ (for Practical Joker), then proceeded to play practical jokes on everyone he could find.

After a while, PJ ran into Lilo and Stitch. When PJ pulled many pranks on Stitch, the latter attempted to beat him up, only to be stopped by Lilo, who pointed out that the annoying prankster was still Stitch's cousin.

PJ was later taken back to the Pelekais' house, where Jumba claimed that PJ's jokes could result in mayhem. Regardless, PJ relentlessly pranked Lilo's strict substitute teacher for her hula class, while unknowingly framing Lilo for it.

Later, Lilo wanted to play a spiteful joke on her harsh substitute teacher, despite Stitch's protests. PJ then pulled some more pranks on Stitch when the latter tried to capture him. However, Stitch eventually scooped PJ in a container so that he wouldn't cause further chaos.

When Stitch refused to allow PJ to help with Lilo's scheme, she used Drowsy to put Stitch to sleep and released PJ from containment for his assistance. PJ then cleverly formulated a plan to humiliate Lilo's substitute teacher with the ultimate practical joke.

Stitch somehow later woke up on his own and triggered a booby trap set up by PJ, resulting in Stitch getting stuck inside PJ's containment orb. The next morning, Stitch was freed from containment by Pleakley. The former then rushed to Lilo's hula school, hoping to prevent her and PJ from pulling their prank.

Meanwhile, PJ waited to unleash his practical joke on Lilo's substitute teacher, which ended up backfiring (due to Stitch intervening), and thus caused PJ to literally get caught in his own trap. Luckily, PJ was freed from his own contraption by a sympathetic Stitch.

Shortly after, PJ found his one true place as an opening act performer for Moses' hula gig.

### Leroy & Stitch

The first 624 experiments, including PJ, were rounded up by Leroy and taken to a stadium to be destroyed. However, Lilo, Stitch, Jumba, Pleakley, Reuben, and Gantu arrived before the experiments could be destroyed.

PJ participated in the following battle between the experiments and the Leroy clones.

The Leroys soon gained the upper hand in the battle but were defeated when Lilo, Stitch, Reuben, and several other experiments performed the song "Aloha ʻOe", which caused the Leroy army to shut down due to the original Leroy's fail-safe. PJ joins them for the song by playing the trumpet.

### Stitch!

PJ made an appearance in the Stitch! anime where he was released by Hämsterviel to play practical jokes around town while disguised as Stitch in order to frame him for the pranks. In "Shogun", it is shown that PJ was dehydrated back into an experiment pod and locked in Jumba's vault. PJ later appeared as one of the experiments who helped Stitch fight Dark End. He also appeared in the special Stitch and the Planet of Sand.
