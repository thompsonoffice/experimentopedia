# Leroy Clones

Clones of Experiment 629 (Leroy) made by Hämsterviel in Leroy & Stitch.

## Appearance

Their appearance is identical to the original Leroy's, except that the original Leroy wears a black uniform while the clones do not.

## Powers and abilities

The clones also share the original Leroy's abilities.

## Weaknesses

Leroy was programmed with a fail-safe during his creation. Since the clones share his abilities and characteristics, they also share his weakness to shut down when they hear the song "Aloha ʻOe".

## Personality

Since they are like the original Leroy, the clones all share his personality, appearance, and abilities. They are vicious and love to cause destruction as much as the original Leroy. They're also cunning and intelligent, as the clones use their numbers to take down the experiments in their fight.

They also share their love for Elvis Presley's music, as they all danced to "Jailhouse Rock" in their prison cells.

The clones are also immune to some of the experiments' powers; since Leroy is created after them, they are immune to Angel's song. However, they're not immune to all of them, as some of them are affected by Spike and Babyfier.
